About This Land Cruiser Prado

Experience unparalleled comfort and commanding road presence with this showroom-condition 2023 Toyota Land Cruiser Prado TXL. Perfectly configured for both premier corporate transit along Bole Road and Kazanchis diplomatic corridors, as well as rugged weekend expeditions into the Great Rift Valley.

Equipped with Toyota’s proven 2.8-liter D-4D turbo diesel engine paired with an intelligent 6-speed automatic gearbox and full-time four-wheel drive. Whether climbing the steep curves to Entoto Natural Park, gliding over cobblestones in historic Piassa, or navigating out-of-town gravel trails toward Wenchi Crater Lake and Bishoftu resorts, this vehicle glides with total composure.

Freshly valeted before every handover. Includes dual auxiliary fuel tanks so you never have to worry about long-distance highway refueling runs outside Addis Ababa municipal zones.
